# Ice's Stack (homepage, project cards, engineer, build)

Mode: Experience. Scope: phase 1, Home card `/`, project cards `/cases/[slug]`, grid `/work`, `/engineer`, `/build`. Old paper pages (services, cv, garden, language pages) are phase 2.

Audience and job: Copenhagen small-business owners (often on a phone) see real shops Ice built and reach "Build mine"; recruiters arrive from the CV at `/engineer`. Success is an enquiry or a call. Proof is the 7 lead projects (Racha, Trailr, Bevisly, MockMate, Lumina, Saep, Millennial) plus the archive (Satoshi, Cinema). Real portrait available, dithered.

Memorable moment: a dithered screenshot developing into full colour, the only colour on the site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a HyperCard stack: a set of linked cards one person made, each card a shipped thing, browsed with painted buttons and a back trail. It refuses the dark developer portfolio with a giant name over a tile grid.

OWN-WORLD: One-bit black on white; greys exist only as stipple dither. Pixel display type (Pixelify Sans) for headlines, menus and buttons; Atkinson Hyperlegible for body. Hand-drawn 3px card borders, stacked card edges, a dithered desk ground, painted round-cornered buttons that invert to solid black when active, a menu bar, a bottom nav bar (back, home, n of N, grid, forward), scrolling fields with a pixel scrollbar, a script window for code.

STORY: The visitor lands on Ice's Home card, sees his dithered portrait and one line, flips through cards, presses a screenshot and watches a client's real site develop into colour, then either fills in the blank Build mine card or opens the Engineer card.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Menu bar across the top. One card centred on the dithered desk at about 75% of the viewport width, stacked edges visible bottom right. Left half: "Ice's Stack" in pixel display, one sentence, availability line, three stacked painted buttons (Projects, Build mine, Engineer). Right half: the dithered portrait in a framed field. Nav bar under the card; Message Box line at the bottom. On phones the card fills the screen, portrait above text, nav bar at the thumb.

FORM: medium-native-hypercard-stack-shoebox, dealt challenger chosen over the assigned list position 6 (Bangkok shophouse street); seed:daf9f1b0d600d1a2. Signature interactions: develop into colour, Message Box chatbot, Build mine as author mode, stipple dissolve between cards.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
